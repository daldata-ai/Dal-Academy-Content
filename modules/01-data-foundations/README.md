## What you will be able to do

- Classify structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data by operational consequences.
- Distinguish Python/pandas dtypes from business meaning and measurement scale.
- Normalize nested JSON, extract features from text, and reason about image tensors.
- Profile schema, nulls, duplicates, ranges, and uniqueness before analysis.
- Design practical data-quality rules and lightweight data contracts.
